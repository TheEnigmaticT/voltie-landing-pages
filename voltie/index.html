The Voltie Pro charger.

A welcoming face. Practical details, from the housing to the way your tenants start a charge.

Durable exterior

A stainless, acid-resistant steel housing with a scratch-resistant, crystal-clear acrylic glass (PMMA) front panel that does not yellow in sunlight.

Charging status

The illuminated smile shows when charging is available, in progress, complete, or needs attention.

Comfortable cable

Choose a fixed 5 m or 7 m cable to suit your parking layout. A Tesla charge-port opener is available on request.

Connector options

The European model comes with a Type 2 cable. Ask about Type 1 options and the right connector for your market.

Informative display

Read charging information on the built-in display, with multiple languages supported.

RFID access

Give tenants and authorized users a straightforward way to start charging with an RFID identifier.

Adjustable mood light

Set the color of the ambient light around the panel to suit your property.

Optional column

No suitable wall? An optional stainless-steel post can support up to two Voltie chargers.

Specifications and options vary by market. We’ll confirm the connector, mounting and installation requirements for your property.

Questions, answered plainly

Before you plan, ask.

Can Voltie work at my location?

Availability, installation scope and local requirements vary. Share your property details and ask what is possible in your location.

Can you help with installation?

Voltie can help plan installation and offer done-for-you installation where available. The exact electrical work and services depend on the project location and scope.

How can tenants access charging?

Discuss RFID and app access for tenant charging. The right setup depends on how you want access to work at the property.

What about payment and incentives?

Drivers may be able to pay directly depending on the project setup. Ask about payment options, local incentives and requirements; no eligibility or incentive outcome is guaranteed.
